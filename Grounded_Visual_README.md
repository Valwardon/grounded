Traditional AI predicts tokens.

Grounded builds understanding from lived experience.

## Philosophy

Grounded doesn't predict language.

It builds an internal model of reality through deterministic interaction with sensors, actions, prediction errors, curiosity, memory, planning, and continual structural growth.

**Not artificial intelligence.**

**Artificial cognition.**
